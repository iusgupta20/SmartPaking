About the Project

Built to solve a real civic problem

Street parking in Indian cities is unmanaged: drivers circle for minutes, meters go unused and administrators have zero visibility. SmartPark 360 attacks all three problems with a single, demonstrable platform.

For drivers: find, reserve and pay for a bay before you even leave home.

For cities: live occupancy, revenue and peak-load analytics to plan better.

For the environment: fewer circling cars means less fuel burn and cleaner air.

Problem Statement

SIH1515 — Smart and Effective Realtime Management of Street Parking

Design a system that monitors street parking in real time, guides citizens to available bays and gives municipal administrators a single command center for operations.

Stack

100% client-side prototype

HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5 and vanilla JavaScript (ES6) — with LocalStorage as the database and mock JSON as the seed data. No servers, no frameworks, fully explainable.
